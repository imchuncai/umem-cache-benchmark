=================
Benchmark-1g-512b
=================

Conclusion
==========
::

	Umem-cache's hit rate is 10% higher than Memcached and 15% higher than Redis.
	Umem-cache's hit throughput is 62% higher than Memcached and 77% higher than Redis.

	Umem-cache's P90   latency is 59% lower than Memcached and 53% lower than Redis.
	Umem-cache's P99.9 latency is 54% lower than Memcached and 53% lower than Redis.
