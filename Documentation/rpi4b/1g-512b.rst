=================
Benchmark-1g-512b
=================

Conclusion
==========
::

	Umem-cache's hit rate is 10% higher than Memcached and 17% higher than Redis.
	Umem-cache's hit throughput is 16% higher than Memcached and 69% higher than Redis.
