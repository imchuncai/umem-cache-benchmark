=================
Benchmark-1g-512b
=================

Conclusion
==========
::

	Umem-cache's hit rate is 9% higher than Memcached and 15% higher than Redis.
	Umem-cache's hit throughput is 13% higher than Memcached and 58% higher than Redis.
