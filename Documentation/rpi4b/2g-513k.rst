=================
Benchmark-2g-513k
=================

Conclusion
==========
::

	Umem-cache's hit rate is 12% higher than Memcached and 12% higher than Redis.
	Umem-cache's hit throughput is 10% higher than Memcached and 9% higher than Redis.

	Note: network throughput is nearing the limit of gigabit networks.
	Note: Redis only used about 80% of the configured memory.
