=================
Benchmark-2g-513k
=================

Conclusion
==========
::

	Umem-cache's hit rate is 11% higher than Memcached and 11% higher than Redis.
	Umem-cache's hit throughput is 6% higher than Memcached and 10% higher than Redis.

	Note: network throughput is nearing the limit of gigabit networks.
	Note: Redis only used 82% of the configured memory.
